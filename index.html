        Before you go

        • Avoid handling the phone while moving. Put it in a mount you can see and check the route before you leave.
        • Keep this tab open and the screen on. With the screen off or in another tab, the browser stops following you.

        Add to home screen

        Open route-gpx from an icon, like an app. It opens in your browser, and saved routes open even without a connection.

        iPhone and iPad

        1. Tap Share: in Safari, at the bottom of the screen or in the ⋯ menu; in Chrome, in the address bar.
        2. Choose Add to Home Screen. Scroll down if you don't see it.
        3. Tap Add.

        Android

        1. Open the browser menu ⋮, at the top right in Chrome.
        2. Choose Add to home screen. In Samsung Internet: Add page to, then Home screen.
        3. Confirm with Add. The phone may ask once more where to put it.
